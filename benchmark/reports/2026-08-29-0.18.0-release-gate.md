### 2026-08-29: 0.18.0 release gate (pluggable store, DuckDB implementation)

Full battery, `node benchmark/release.mjs` (compare + 13k/26k scale + stress + both quality
evals), one sitting, Apple Silicon, Node 26.7.0, still on 0.17.1's number per RELEASING.md
step 2; the 0.18.0 bump happened after the sitting. First full-battery sitting on `chunk:v5`
(mdast chunking, 0.17.0) and on the store line: the backing-store interface, the DuckDB
implementation (fts BM25 lexical, native `array_cosine_similarity` vectors, on-first-use
install), 927 passing. Every corpus and the default model were already cached
(`.tmp/cache`, `~/.sense`); nothing fetched fresh this sitting.

**Scope note, for the next reader.** This sitting is the default store's (sqlite) gate.
`release.mjs --store duckdb`'s gate is the tree battery alone (no npm baseline predates the
store, and the quality evals are store-independent), and it did not run this sitting. The
duckdb store's correctness stands on the store-parity suite and the two 2026-08-29 reports
(lexical, vectors); two-store columns over time land when compare.mjs gains its store loop,
and that sitting fills both.

`--version` canary: 20 ms across 5 runs (one at 30 ms), unchanged from the 20-30 ms
invariant: mdast and the language distribution (the 0.17.1 lazy-load) stay off the
CLI-startup path.

(-3.4%); the correlated wall bulk row moves the other way (1733 to 1188 ms). Called as
variance on once-measured rows, not averaged away: if the same two rows move together again
next sitting, that is a per-call cost in open/reconcile to investigate, not a noise
judgement renewed. The 13k freshness row (72.5 to 82.9 ms, +14.3%) is the same call: its
update-1-file twin moved +3.6% and its cold build -4.9%.

again; the in-process cold builds under them are flat (-4.9%, -3.4%).
